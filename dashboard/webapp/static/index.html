IPSSI Paris · Data Lake project

A digital twin of Paris, Lyon and Lille

UrbanHub collects weather, bike-share and air-quality data for three French cities, stores it in a Data Lake, and joins it hour by hour. Every chart on this site is a live SQL query on the lake: change the city or the period and the database answers again.

How it's computed

For each city and calendar month, the database computes the normal daily temperature and its standard deviation. A day further than the chosen z-score is abnormal, and consecutive abnormal days of the same kind form a period (a "gaps and islands" query).

Is there a link between weather and visibility?

How it's computed

Hourly rows where visibility is known, grouped by rain and wind bands. Fog means visibility under 1 km. Visibility is only available in part of the weather table, so the answer says how many hours it is based on.
